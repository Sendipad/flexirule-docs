# Core Actions

Actions are the building blocks of your FlexiRule workflows. Each action performs a specific task, from checking conditions to sending emails or updating records.

Explore the guide for each action below to learn how to use it effectively.

## Data & Records
- **[Set Value]({{< relref "set-value.md" >}})**: Change fields on your document or manage temporary variables.
- **[Update Record]({{< relref "update-record.md" >}})**: Create, update, or delete other documents in the system.
- **[Query Records]({{< relref "query-records.md" >}})**: Find and retrieve information from your database.

## Logic & Flow
- **[Check]({{< relref "check.md" >}})**: Split your logic into "True" and "False" paths based on conditions.
- **[Switch]({{< relref "switch.md" >}})**: Direct your flow into multiple different paths based on a value.
- **[Repeat]({{< relref "repeat.md" >}})**: Perform a series of steps for every item in a list.
- **[Wait]({{< relref "wait.md" >}})**: Pause the execution of your rule for a set amount of time.
- **[Stop / Error]({{< relref "stop-error.md" >}})**: End the rule execution or display an error message to the user.

## Communication
- **[Notify]({{< relref "notify-action.md" >}})**: Send emails, system alerts, and UI messages.

## Advanced Orchestration
- **[Process]({{< relref "process.md" >}})**: Trigger more complex system operations or other rules.
- **[Start]({{< relref "start.md" >}})**: The entry point of every rule.
