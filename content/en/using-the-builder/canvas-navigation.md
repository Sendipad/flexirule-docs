# Navigating the Canvas

The Rule Builder is your visual workspace. It's where you design your business processes by connecting different logic blocks together.

## Basic Controls

- **Move Around**: Click and drag any empty space on the canvas to move the view.
- **Zoom In and Out**: Use your mouse wheel to zoom.
- **Select a Block**: Click on any block to select it and see its settings.
- **Move a Block**: Click and drag a block to move it to a new spot.
- **Delete a Block**: Select a block and press `Delete` or `Backspace`.

## Connecting Blocks

To define the flow of your process, you connect blocks with lines:
1. Find the output port (a small circle) on the side of a block.
2. Click and drag from that port to the input port of another block.
3. For blocks like **Check (Condition)**, you'll have "Yes" (Green) and "No" (Red) ports to handle different outcomes.

## Shortcuts for Speed

- **Copy and Paste**: Select blocks and use `Ctrl+C` and `Ctrl+V`.
- **Multi-Select**: Hold `Shift` and drag a box around multiple blocks to move or copy them together.

## Seeing Your Logic in Action

The canvas isn't just for design; it's also for testing.

- **Test Run**: Click the **Test Run** button to see how your rule behaves with real data.
- **Follow the Path**: After a test, the path taken by the logic will light up on the canvas, showing you exactly what happened.
- **Check the Details**: Click on any block after a test to see the specific values it processed.
