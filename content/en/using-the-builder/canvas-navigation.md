# Canvas Navigation

The Rule Builder is your visual workspace for designing logic. It provides an easy-to-use canvas where you can build workflows by arranging and connecting blocks.

## Navigating the Canvas

- **Move Around**: Click and drag any empty space on the canvas to pan.
- **Zoom In/Out**: Use your mouse wheel or the zoom buttons on the screen to change your view.
- **Center View**: Click the 'Fit to Screen' button to instantly see your entire rule.

## Working with Blocks

- **Select**: Click on a block to see its settings in the sidebar.
- **Move**: Click and drag a block to move it to a new position.
- **Delete**: Select a block and press the `Delete` key on your keyboard. The system will try to reconnect your flow automatically.

## Testing Your Logic

You don't have to wait for a real event to see if your rule works.

- **Test Run**: Click the **Test Run** button to simulate how your rule will behave with real data.
- **Visual Path**: After a test, the canvas will light up in green to show you exactly which path the logic followed.
- **Step Details**: Click on any block after a test to see what happened inside that specific step (for example, what values were changed).
