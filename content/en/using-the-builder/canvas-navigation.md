# Navigating the Canvas

The Rule Builder is a visual workspace where you design your automation. It is designed to be intuitive, but understanding a few key terms and controls will help you build faster.

## Key Terminology

### Blocks
Every step in your automation is represented by a **Block**. Think of blocks as individual tasks, like "Check a value", "Send an email", or "Wait for 2 days".

### Connections
The lines between blocks are **Connections**. They define the order in which tasks are performed.
- **Standard Connection**: A simple flow from one task to the next.
- **True/False Connections**: Used by "Check" blocks to branch your logic based on whether a condition is met.

### Ports
The circles on the edges of blocks are **Ports**.
- **Input Port** (Left side): Where the flow enters the block.
- **Output Port** (Right side): Where the flow exits to the next block.

## Moving Around

- **Pan**: Click and drag any empty space on the canvas to move around.
- **Zoom**: Use your mouse wheel or the zoom buttons in the toolbar to zoom in and out.
- **Fit to Screen**: Click the "Center" button in the toolbar to see your entire rule at once.

## Managing Blocks

### Adding Blocks
The easiest way to add a block is via the **Action Zone**. Hover your mouse over any connection point or empty space near a block to see the **+** icon. Clicking this will let you pick a new block to insert.

### Editing Blocks
Click on any block to open its **Configuration Panel**. This is where you set the specific details for that task (e.g., the email recipient or the condition to check).

### Deleting Blocks
Select a block and press the `Delete` or `Backspace` key on your keyboard, or use the trash icon in the block's header.

### Moving Blocks
Simply click and drag a block to reposition it. Connections will automatically adjust to follow the block.
