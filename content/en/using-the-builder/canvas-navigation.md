# Canvas Navigation

The FlexiRule canvas is your workspace for designing automations. It is a flexible, infinite space where you can arrange your logic blocks.

## Moving Around

-   **Panning**: Click and drag any empty space on the canvas to move your view.
-   **Zooming**: Use your mouse wheel or trackpad pinch gesture to zoom in and out. This helps you focus on specific details or see the entire rule at once.
-   **Fit to View**: Click the "Fit to View" icon in the toolbar to automatically center and zoom your logic flow so it fits perfectly on your screen.

## Interacting with Blocks

-   **Selecting**: Click on a block to select it. This will open its configuration panel on the right side of the screen.
-   **Moving**: Click and drag a block to reposition it on the canvas. Connections will automatically follow.
-   **Deleting**: Select a block and press the `Delete` or `Backspace` key on your keyboard, or use the trash icon in the block's header.

## Connection Ports

Each block has "Ports" used to link it to other blocks:
-   **Input Port**: Usually on the left or top; this is where the execution flow enters the block.
-   **Output Port**: Usually on the right or bottom; this is where the flow continues to the next block.
-   **True/False Ports**: Specialized output ports on Check blocks that determine the path based on whether a condition is met.

## Toolbar Controls

The toolbar at the top of the builder provides quick access to essential tools:
-   **Save**: Saves your current progress.
-   **Test Run**: Allows you to simulate the rule with real data.
-   **Auto-Layout**: Automatically arranges your blocks in a clean, readable structure.
-   **Undo/Redo**: Quickly reverse or re-apply changes.
