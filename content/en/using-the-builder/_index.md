# Using the Rule Builder

The Rule Builder is the heart of FlexiRule. It's a visual workspace where you can design your business logic by arranging and connecting different blocks.

## Key Concepts

- **Canvas**: The infinite workspace where you build your rules.
- **Blocks**: Individual steps or actions (like "Check", "Set Value", or "Notify").
- **Connections**: Lines that define the flow of your logic from one block to another.
- **Action Zone**: The interactive area around blocks and connections where you can add new steps.

## Navigating the Workspace

Building a rule is as simple as dragging and dropping. You can move around the canvas, zoom in to see details, or zoom out to see your entire workflow at once.

[Learn more about Canvas Navigation]({{< relref "canvas-navigation.md" >}})

## Adding and Managing Steps

To build your logic, you'll add various "Actions" to your canvas. Each action performs a specific task, and you can configure them to fit your business needs.
