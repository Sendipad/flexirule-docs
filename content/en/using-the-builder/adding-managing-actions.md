# Adding and Managing Actions

To build your business logic, you will add various "Actions" (blocks) to your canvas.

## Adding a New Step

The easiest way to add a step is through the **Action Zone**.

1. **Hover**: Move your mouse over any connection line or the empty space after a block.
2. **Click**: Click the `+` icon that appears.
3. **Select**: A menu will pop up. You can search for the action you want (like "Notify" or "Check").
4. **Configure**: Click on the new block to set it up in the sidebar.

## Connecting Your Logic

The lines between blocks show the order in which they will run.

- **Automatic Connection**: When you add a new block using the `+` icon, it's automatically connected for you.
- **Manual Connection**: You can create your own connections by clicking and dragging from the circle on one block to another.
- **Smart Reconnect**: If you delete a block in the middle of a chain, the builder will automatically connect the remaining blocks to keep your flow intact.

## Configuration Sidebar

When you click on a block, you'll see its settings on the right side of the screen.

- **Simple Settings**: We use clear language to help you set up each action quickly.
- **Helpful Suggestions**: When choosing fields or values, the builder shows you what's available so you don't have to guess.
- **Real-time Checks**: The builder will let you know immediately if a required setting is missing.
