# Adding and Managing Blocks

Building a rule is like putting together a puzzle. You add **Blocks** and connect them to create a workflow.

## The Action Zone

The **Action Zone** is the quickest way to build your rule. It appears whenever you want to add a new step.

### How to Add a Block
1. **Find a Connection Point**: Hover your mouse over the "output" circles on any existing block.
2. **Click the Plus**: Click the `+` icon that appears.
3. **Search & Select**: A menu will pop up. You can browse through categories like "Data" or "Communication," or simply type what you're looking for (e.g., "Notify").
4. **Automatic Connection**: Once you select a block, it is added to the canvas and automatically connected to the previous step.

## Connecting Blocks

### Automatic Healing
If you delete a block that was in the middle of a flow, FlexiRule is smart enough to "heal" the connection. It will automatically connect the previous block to the next one so your rule doesn't break.

### Manual Connections
You can always create your own connections:
1. Click and hold on a connection point (circle) of a block.
2. Drag the line to the connection point of another block.
3. Release the mouse to create the link.

## Configuring a Block

When you click on a block, a settings panel opens on the right side of the screen.

- **Required Fields**: Any fields that *must* be filled out will be highlighted.
- **Smart Suggestions**: When you are picking a field or a variable, FlexiRule only shows you the options that actually make sense for that block.
- **Save Changes**: Your changes in the settings panel are saved automatically to the canvas.
