# Introduction to FlexiRule

FlexiRule is a **Visual Automation Engine** built for the Frappe Framework and ERPNext. It allows you to design and manage complex business logic using a visual canvas instead of writing scattered code.

## Why FlexiRule?

Traditionally, customizing business logic in ERPNext required writing Python code in "hooks" or "server scripts." As your system grows, this logic becomes difficult to track, debug, and maintain. FlexiRule centralizes this logic into an intuitive, visual layer.

### Key Benefits
*   **Visual Logic:** Build your business flows on a drag-and-drop canvas.
*   **Fast Changes:** Update your business rules in minutes without needing a developer or a system redeploy.
*   **Clear Visibility:** See exactly why a rule ran and what path it took through your logic.
*   **Safe & Secure:** Built-in protections prevent infinite loops and ensure your system stays stable.

## How it Works

1.  **Define a Trigger:** Tell FlexiRule when to run (e.g., "When a Sales Order is saved").
2.  **Build the Flow:** Use blocks to check conditions, set values, or send notifications.
3.  **Test & Activate:** Verify your logic with built-in testing tools and go live with one click.

Whether you're automating a simple discount or a complex multi-stage approval process, FlexiRule provides a professional, visual way to manage your business logic.
