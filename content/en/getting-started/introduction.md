# Introduction to FlexiRule

FlexiRule is a **Visual Automation Platform** for ERPNext and Frappe. It allows you to design and manage your business logic visually, making your workflows easier to understand, audit, and change.

## Why use FlexiRule?

In many systems, business rules are hidden inside complex code. This makes it hard for business users to understand how the system works and even harder to change it. FlexiRule brings that logic to the surface.

- **See Your Logic**: Rules are built on a visual canvas. You can literally see the path your data takes.
- **Easy to Change**: Need to update a discount rule or an approval path? Just move a few blocks on the canvas.
- **Traceable**: Every time a rule runs, you can see exactly which steps were taken. No more guessing why a record was updated.

## Key Concepts for Users

### Rules
A **Rule** is a collection of steps (blocks) that perform a specific business task. For example, "Calculate Shipping Costs" or "Validate Customer Credit".

### Blocks (Actions)
Each step in a rule is a **Block**. Some blocks check conditions (e.g., "Is the order over $500?"), some retrieve data, and others perform actions like sending an email or updating a record.

### The Canvas
The **Canvas** is your workspace where you drag, drop, and connect blocks to build your automation.
