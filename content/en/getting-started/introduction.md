# Introduction to FlexiRule

FlexiRule is a visual automation platform for Frappe and ERPNext. It allows you to design and manage your business logic on a clear, interactive canvas rather than writing complex code.

## Why use FlexiRule?

Traditionally, customizing business logic in ERP systems involves writing Python scripts and hooks that are often hidden across different files. This makes it hard to see the big picture and even harder to debug when things go wrong.

FlexiRule solves this by centralizing your logic:
- **Visual Builder**: Design your workflows using simple blocks and connections.
- **Easy to Understand**: Anyone on your team can look at a rule and understand what it does.
- **Safe and Fast**: Built-in checks ensure your logic runs correctly without slowing down your system.

## How it Works

Instead of writing code, you create **Rules**. Each rule consists of a series of **Actions** (blocks) connected together. When an event happens—like a new Sales Order being created—FlexiRule follows the path you've designed to perform tasks automatically.

Whether you need to send an email, update a field, or perform a complex calculation, FlexiRule gives you the tools to do it visually.
