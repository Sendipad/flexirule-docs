# What is FlexiRule?

FlexiRule is a **Visual Automation Platform** designed for Frappe and ERPNext. It allows you to build, manage, and understand your business logic using a visual canvas instead of hidden Python code.

## The Problem: Fragmented Logic

In many systems, business rules are scattered across various custom apps, server scripts, and UI scripts. This makes it difficult to see the "big picture"—it's hard to know which rule runs first, why a record changed, or how to update a process without breaking something else.

## The Solution: Visual Orchestration

FlexiRule centralizes your logic into an intuitive, visual interface. Instead of writing code, you connect **Blocks** on a canvas to define exactly how your business should operate.

-   **Visual & Transparent**: See the entire flow of your logic at a glance.
-   **Easy to Manage**: Update your rules in real-time without complex deployments.
-   **Safe & Reliable**: Built-in safeguards ensure your automations run predictably and don't cause system errors.

## How it Works

FlexiRule sits alongside your existing ERPNext setup. You define **Rules** that are triggered by specific events (like saving a Sales Order or a scheduled time). Each Rule consists of a series of blocks that perform tasks like checking conditions, updating values, or sending notifications.

Whether you're automating a simple reminder or a complex multi-step approval process, FlexiRule gives you the tools to build it visually and keep it organized.
